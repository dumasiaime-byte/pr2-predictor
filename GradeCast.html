What's the Purpose?

This application was developed as part of our research paper entitled “Selected Grade 11 Subjects as Predictor on the Academic Performance in Practical Research 2.”

Our study examines how students’ grades in English for Academic and Professional Purposes (EAPP), Practical Research 1, General Mathematics, Statistics and Probability, and Oral Communication can help predict their performance in Practical Research 2 (PR²).

This application uses the regression equation from the research to calculate a predicted PR² grade based on the grades entered in these five subjects. It is intended for educational and research purposes only and is not an official grading system or a replacement for teachers’ assessments.
